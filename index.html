G-Buffer Recording

Press C to start recording your camera path, press C again to stop. Then generate 6 G-buffer video passes (RGB, albedo, depth, normal, metallic, roughness) included in your save .zip.

Panorama Capture

Press P to capture a 360 equirectangular panorama (8K) from your current position. Generates two versions per room: bare (untextured) and furnished (with white floor). Saved in the .zip under panoramas/.
